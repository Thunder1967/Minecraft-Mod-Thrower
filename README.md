> "Throwing is the primal spark of human tactical dominance."

As the most ancient form of long-range combat, the art of throwing once redefined the survival of mankind. The Thrower Mod brings this raw power to your fingertips.
With the specialized "Combat Gauntlets," you are no longer bound by tradition. Why craft a bow when everything in your inventory is a projectile?
- Arsenal of Everything : Throw any vanilla item with precision and force.
- Reactive Impact : Certain items trigger unique effects upon impact (e.g., TNT explodes on contact).
- Custom Enchantments : Empower your Gauntlets with exclusive enchantments to push the limits of velocity and destruction.

投擲是人類習得的最強能力之一，作為最原始的遠程攻擊手段"投擲"大幅增強了人類的戰鬥力和生存能力。
在這個模組中你可以使用"手套"來投擲所有Minecraft原版物品。
- 有些物品有特殊的擊中效果(如: TNT->落地爆炸)
- 手套專屬附魔
